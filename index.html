About Pawn Playground

Nurturing Young Pawns into Resilient Thinkers

Founded in Shah Alam to provide structured, supportive chess mentorship where every child learns to calculate deeply and play with joy.

Founder's Journey

"Chess teaches young minds that every choice has a consequence."

Welcome to Pawn Playground, the premier destination for young pawns to start and elevate their chess journey. My name is Iskandar Hakimi, and I am the founder and head coach of Pawn Playground.

I have been deeply passionate about chess since I was a young child. I have always believed that it is a game with the rare power to bring people together while developing irreplaceable cognitive skills: critical thinking, disciplined decision-making, and long-term strategic foresight.

Over the years, I have competed in numerous competitive tournaments, studied with esteemed coaches, and developed a modern, encouraging coaching syllabus. Whether your child has never touched a piece or is preparing for MSSD district selection, our team is here to guide them step-by-step.

Our Training Pillars

How We Train Champions

A three-pronged coaching methodology engineered to cultivate practical tournament strength.

Pillar 1

Structured Chess Lessons

Expert-led instruction guiding students from basic piece dynamics and fundamental checkmate patterns up to sophisticated opening repertoires and endgame conversions through interactive master studies.

Pillar 2

Post-Mortem Game Analysis

We provide each student with personalized feedback on their live tournament and practice games, pinpointing critical tactical oversights and replacing guesswork with structured calculation.

Pillar 3

Knowledge Sharing & Community

We cultivate a supportive ecosystem connecting coaches, enthusiastic young peers, and dedicated chess parents, sharing curated resources, tournament announcements, and continuous encouragement.

Coaching Programs & Pathways

Three progressive developmental stages designed for students in Shah Alam and online. Select a program below to inquire directly with our coaching team.

Stage 1

Beginner Foundation

Target Level: Ages 5–12 • New to chess or basic moves

  • ✓ Master legal piece movements, board coordinates, special moves (castling, en passant), and checkmate fundamentals.
  • ✓ Develop board vision, prevent 1-move blunders, and learn primary tactical motifs (forks, pins, skewers).
  • ✓ Build over-the-board concentration, tournament etiquette, sportsmanship, and confidence in decision-making.
